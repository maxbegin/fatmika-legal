Delete your account and data

Applies to NonoPix (app.nono.daily)

NonoPix creates an anonymous account automatically so your progress survives reinstalls. You can delete that account — and everything tied to it — at any time.

How to request deletion

Email maxime.begin@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my NonoPix account".

  • If you signed in with Google or Apple, send the request from (or mention) the email address on that account so we can find it.
  • If you never signed in (anonymous account), open the app, go to Profile, and include the information shown there so we can identify your account — or simply uninstall the app: an anonymous account that is never used again is deleted automatically after 12 months of inactivity.

What gets deleted

Everything tied to the account, within 14 days of the request:

  • Solve history (times, errors, hints, dates) and streak data
  • Profile row (display name, friend groups, preferences)
  • Usage events
  • Push notification token
  • The account itself (Supabase auth user)

Purchase state lives with RevenueCat and your Google Play / App Store account; refunds and purchase management happen through the store.

Data kept

None. We keep no advertising identifiers and no marketing lists, so there is nothing else to remove. See the full privacy policy.
